    Method

    For every year, a harmonic regression (trend + annual and semi-annual terms) is fitted to the Sentinel-2 NBR of the three preceding years. Scenes in the April–November season whose residual drops below −2.5 σ are flagged; a pixel becomes an alert when flagged in at least two scenes, lies in core forest (ESA WorldCover + NDVI > 0.5) and was not already declining.

    Alerts are grouped into 8-connected patches of at least 0.3 ha, vectorised in Earth Engine and split by NP zone. Pixels alerted in an earlier year are not reported again.
